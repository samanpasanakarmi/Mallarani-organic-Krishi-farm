About Our Farm

Mallarani Organic Krisha Farm is dedicated to growing fresh, natural, and chemical-free food for families who care about healthy living. We believe in traditional farming, clean soil, and honest food.

Our goal is to connect people with nature by providing healthy farm products grown with care, patience, and respect for the environment.

Meet Our Farm Team

Mallarani Organic Krisha Farm is managed by Mr. Gopal Prasad Nakarmi and family with care, dedication, and respect for natural farming.

Our family works every day to grow fresh products while protecting the soil, supporting nature, and serving our local community with trust.
